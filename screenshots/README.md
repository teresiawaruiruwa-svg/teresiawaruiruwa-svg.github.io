# Screenshot placeholders

Add redacted screenshots to this directory and update the corresponding placeholder panels in `index.html`.

| Figure | Portfolio section | Suggested filename | Capture guidance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home / professional branding | `01-home.png` | Top fold with name, headline, navigation, and portrait |
| 2 | Resume / qualifications | `02-resume.png` | Education, experience, and certifications |
| 3 | Projects showcase | `03-projects.png` | Three project cards and repository links |
| 4 | Labs / challenges | `04-labs.png` | Lab challenge summaries and write-up links |
| 5 | Technical writing | `05-writing.png` | Featured article or published writing |
| 6 | Contact / footer | `06-contact.png` | Email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and footer |

Use browser screenshots at a consistent viewport. Crop unnecessary browser chrome where appropriate. Before committing, redact flags, credentials, tokens, VPN/account identifiers, and sensitive target details. Never use fabricated screenshots as evidence.
